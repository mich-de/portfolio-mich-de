Does the new GPT-6.2 actually beat Fable 5.2? Practical tests, costs, and performance compared

The generative artificial intelligence landscape has entered a new round of heavy competition at the top. OpenAI has officially released its new models — GPT-6.2 Astra (the high-end, highly intelligent flagship) and GPT-6.2 Sol (the daily-use, cost-optimized variant) — directly challenging Anthropic's benchmark standard, Fable 5.2.

In tests conducted by Simone Rizzo and independent evaluations by Artificial Analysis, the new models aim to disrupt the market not only by delivering superior performance, but also by introducing sharp optimizations in operating costs and execution times.

1. Benchmarks and Economic Claims: What Actually Changes?

In OpenAI's presentation metrics, the new GPT-6.2 models rank exceptionally high in long-duration reasoning tests (such as Terminal Bench, Science Index, and Agent Lux Exam). However, the real breakthrough emphasized by Sam Altman's company is the ratio between intelligence and cost: while matching or outperforming Fable 5.2, task costs and processing times drop dramatically (reducing by a factor of 5x to 20x depending on the reasoning effort configured).

Yet, looking at independent aggregator datasets, the picture is more nuanced:

  • Market Standing: independent benchmarks initially place GPT-6.2 Astra in second position, right behind Fable 5.2, followed by Claude Opus 5 and the Sol variant.
  • The Savings Paradox: although the per-token cost is noticeably lower (priced at $8 input / $40 output for Astra, and $2/$10 for Sol), increased efficiency often prompts users to delegate more complex, longer tasks, partially offsetting the actual savings on token spending.

2. Field Testing: Head-to-Head Video Game Development

To verify company claims, a direct practical test was performed in the video by feeding the exact same prompt to both GPT-6.2 Astra and Fable 5.2: building an aviation-themed pixel art video game from scratch featuring interactive controls, cockpit views, and missile launching mechanics.

The field comparison revealed significant differences:

  • Execution Speed: GPT-6.2 Astra completed the task in just 13 minutes and 15 seconds, whereas Fable 5.2 took 49 minutes (nearly four times longer).
  • Output Quality: from a graphical and gameplay perspective, both models produced an excellent, stable result that strictly followed instructions, showing that final quality is essentially tied.

3. Security, Restrictions, and Self-Improving Risks

A core focus of the release revolves around cybersecurity and model alignment. OpenAI has implemented stringent protective guardrails:

  • Advanced cybersecurity and exploit-writing capabilities have been restricted and made accessible only to vetted organizations through the Open Day Break program.
  • For the first time, an OpenAI model has reached the high-risk threshold for self-improving capabilities, demonstrating the potential to accelerate AI research and software architecture design. This has prompted the company to step up cloud container monitoring to prevent misuse or autonomous code escapes.

[ tip ]

Although GPT-6.2 Astra proves extraordinarily fast and cost-effective in coding and simulation tasks, the X and Reddit developer community remains divided: while execution speed is widely praised, some engineers note that the model occasionally tends to "over-engineer" solutions, complicating code that could be written much more linearly.
